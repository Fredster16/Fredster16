## Part 1: How the challenge works

**The premise:** you get one Deira Clo cap onto **[TARGET DJ]** through six people. Each person has to introduce you to the next, and you meet every one of them yourself.

### The two caps
- **The main cap.** It travels with you the whole way. Every link puts it on for the same framed shot, and it ends on [TARGET DJ]'s head and stays with them. It's the series prop and the final proof (Foad's payoff is always a proof object: the timer, his notes, his battery, [F3](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcG7iWpNJLz/)).
- **Gift caps.** Each link gets their own cap to keep. That's 5 gift caps plus the main cap, and spares. Cost: **[FACT NEEDED: cost per cap]**.

### Rules (said once in the intro, then on screen)
1. Each link introduces you to the next person, and the next one has to be closer to [TARGET DJ].
2. You meet every link in person. No one else films for you.
3. A link only counts once you've met them and they've worn the main cap on camera.

### Clock and stakes
- **Deadline: [DATE].** The best deadline is [TARGET DJ]'s next UK gig, because then the finale has a place and a date. Landen's series ran on "$100k by the beginning of next month" ([L4](https://www.tiktok.com/@landenlevine1/video/7673966554962267423)).
- **Forfeit: [FORFEIT].** You pick it. It needs to be something you can film within 48 hours of missing the deadline, and something viewers want to see more than you want to do it. One direction costs you (e.g. caps given away to commenters); the other is embarrassing. Your call.
- **Dead ends:** a link who can't or won't introduce you doesn't count against the six, but it burns days off the clock. Dead ends get their own short episode. That's your honest beat, like Luke losing to three eight-year-olds ([P2](https://www.instagram.com/p/DXkFxLPAsR_/)).

### The counter (in every episode, from the first frame)
`LINK 2/6 · 19 DAYS LEFT` sits at the top of the screen, the same job as Landen's `Day 24 · $64,804/$100k` ([L1](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbjJuQTvAgZ/)). Links go up while days go down. That's two meters, and the gap between them is the tension.

### How six degrees actually gets you there (planning tool, not facts)
Plan backwards from [TARGET DJ] and forwards from you, and aim for the routes to meet. A likely shape:

| Link | Likely role | Why this hop works |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Someone you know with a foot in nightlife (bar staff, door, student rep, a mate who DJs) | Your world into club world |
| 2 | A local promoter or club resident | They know who books who |
| 3 | A promoter or booker who brings bigger acts to [CITY] | They've dealt with agents |
| 4 | Someone who's worked a show with [TARGET DJ] (support act, tour manager, photographer, agency) | Has been in the same room |
| 5 | [TARGET DJ]'s inner circle (manager, regular b2b partner, mate) | Can actually ask them |
| 6 | [TARGET DJ] | Handoff, ideally at [DATE]'s gig |

The real chain will look different. This is just so you know what to ask each link for: "who do you know who's one step closer?"

### Filming solo (you're the protagonist)
Kit: your phone on a small tripod or clamp, a clip-on mic for the meetings, and a wide lens if you've got one (most of Landen's shots are wide, handheld or mounted). Get a "yes" on camera from every person before you film them, and blur phone numbers and DMs.

| Shot | Every episode | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival walk-and-talk | yes | Selfie to camera, sets up who you're meeting |
| The ask | yes | Tripod wide, both of you in frame |
| **The handshake shot** | yes | Main cap on their head. **Same framing every time** (chest-up, centred, counter on top). In the finale, all six go back to back |
| Gift cap handover | yes | Your point of view |
| The intro to the next link | yes | Phone on speaker or a screen recording of the text (blurred) |
| Exit recap | yes | To camera, one line, counter ticks |

### Series map
- **Ep 0:** the intro (scripted below).
- **Ep 1–5:** one link each (template in Part 3).
- **Setback episodes:** whenever a dead end happens, about 25s each.
- **Ep 6, the finale:** meeting [TARGET DJ], the handshake shot, all six shots back to back, `LINK 6/6`.
- **The forfeit episode:** only if you miss [DATE].

Cadence: Landen posted 50 TikToks in about 80 days (21 Jul–8 Oct). Post each link when it lands, and post setbacks in between so the day counter never goes quiet.

## Part 2: Ep 0, the intro script

### A. Patterns used
- **A persistent counter from the first frame.** Landen's `Day 24 · $/100k` ([L1](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbjJuQTvAgZ/)) becomes `LINK 0/6 · [X] DAYS LEFT`.
- **The "why" beat with real numbers.** Landen's backstory cut, where he explains why he's living in his car ([L1c](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dbl_0Gfy3NS/)), becomes your DM numbers, then "fuck it".
- **A stakes declaration.** Luke's "I'm not leaving this room until…" ([P4](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeFuED_C2wi/)) becomes the deadline and forfeit.
- **Comment bait as a prediction.** Landen's poll ([L7](https://www.tiktok.com/@landenlevine1/video/7681018539167223071), 3,022 comments) and Devin's caption question ([D2](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdR8sFvNF77/)) become "comment how many people you reckon".
- **Ending on the next episode.** Devin ends on a clip of the next day ([D1](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdZpD0UxjNZ/)); you end on knocking at Link 1's door.

### B. Hooks (each one goes out as its own Trial Reel)

### E. Ending / CTA
- **Series follow call:** "Follow if you want to see if it gets there." Every reference account ends by selling the next episode.
- **Prediction comment bait:** "Comment how many people you reckon it'll take." It's numeric, low effort and easy to answer, and you can pin the closest guess in the finale.
- **Cut on the knock, before the door opens.** Link 1's episode picks up right there.

### F. Caption and hashtags
> Day 0. Six introductions to get a Deira Clo cap onto [TARGET DJ] before [DATE]. How many will it actually take?

`#sixdegreesofseparation #viralseries #[TARGETDJTAG] #ukdj #capbrand`
(#viralseries is the tag Devin and Luke use on their series posts, e.g. [D2](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdR8sFvNF77/) and [P2](https://www.instagram.com/p/DXkFxLPAsR_/).)

**TikTok:** post the winning Instagram hook. If the top two Instagram hooks finish close, post the runner-up as a separate TikTok a few days later. Landen's Instagram winner did 0.93× on TikTok while his other cut did 6.0× ([L1](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbjJuQTvAgZ/) vs [L1c](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dbl_0Gfy3NS/)).

### G. Runtime
- Spoken words (H1 + bridge + body, no optional slot, placeholders estimated): **≈140 words**.
- At the research average of **2.77 words/sec** (24 talking outliers): **≈51s**, plus about 3s of deliberate pauses ("So. Fuck it." and the beat on the DM numbers), so **≈54s**. With the optional "why them" slot: ≈57s.
- Target length: **59.8s**, the median of all 32 outliers (Landen's median is 61.8s).
- For reference, at Landen's 4.99 words/sec the same script would run about 29s. Don't try to match his speed. Keep your pace and let the cuts carry the energy.

## Part 3: Episode template for Links 1–5 (about 55–60s each)

| Time | Beat | Spoken (structure only) | On-screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Hook. Rotate the type each episode | The new link, or what's at stake | `LINK n/6 · [X] DAYS LEFT` |
| 3–7s | Bridge: the handoff | "[Previous link] put me onto [this person]." | Previous link's handshake shot, 0.5s |
| 7–18s | Who they are and why they're one step closer, with a number | e.g. how many years they've worked [venue], or who they've booked | The role in the chain graphic |
| 18–30s | The meet: arrival and the ask | The ask, on camera | Wide, tripod |
| 30–38s | Complication / honest beat | What nearly didn't happen | – |
| 38–46s | **Payoff (~70–80%):** main cap on, handshake shot, gift cap | – | Counter ticks to `n/6` |
| 46–54s | Cliffhanger: who's next, the **role** only, no name | e.g. "someone who's [connection to TARGET DJ]" | Next `?` turns into a role |
| 54–60s | CTA | Follow + days left | Counter |

**Setback episode (dead end), about 25s, in Devin and Foad's short format:** the dead end as the hook (0–3s), what happened (3–15s), the new route (15–22s), counter unchanged and days down (22–25s).
