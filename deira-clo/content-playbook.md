## 1. Content funnel

At about 1.2K TikTok followers and 300 on Instagram, built to sell the 28 grey caps.

### TOFU — get found by lads who've never heard of you

- **Job:** reach new UK lads aged 14–30 through humour or relatability, with the cap in shot but never pitched.
- **After watching:** "that's me and my mates." They laugh, send it to a mate, and follow.
- **Ratio: about 65% (9 of 14 posts a week).** Under 10K followers, reach is the bottleneck: no one buys from an account they've never seen. Skits averaged 8.8x their account median in the teardown, the biggest lever.
- **Ideas:**
  1. Five-a-side changing room: the whole team, ref included, turns up in the grey.
  2. Rate the Fit: street interview, the grey cap goes on, the score goes up.
  3. Cap roast rap battle: two lads trade bars about each other's caps.
- **Biggest mistake:** making it an advert. Plain "new drop" posts topped out at 1.6–2.3x in the teardown, while skits where the product was only the punchline hit 22x.

### MOFU — make the cap worth £28

- **Job:** turn a follower into someone who trusts the quality and feels part of the crew.
- **After watching:** "not just a market cap. I'd wear that." They save it, comment, or check the profile.
- **Ratio: about 20% (3 a week).** Price is your number one objection, so it needs steady answers. More than this and the feed reads as a sales page before people know you.
- **Ideas:**
  1. £8 market cap vs £28 Deira cap: stitching, crown, inside, tested on camera.
  2. "Would you model for Deira?" casting call (12x for Bulley).
  3. Founder reacts to a real 2am order notification.
- **Biggest mistake:** dodging price. Competitors' comments are full of "too expensive" and nobody answers. Say £28 out loud, then show why.

### BOFU — get the tap on the link in bio

- **Job:** give people who already want it a reason to buy today.
- **After watching:** "if I don't buy now, it's gone." They tap the link.
- **Ratio: about 15% (2 a week).** That's above the usual 10% because 28 units in 90 days needs just over 2 sales a week, and real scarcity only lands if you keep repeating it.
- **Ideas:**
  1. 28 Made countdown: overhead shelf shot, real units left.
  2. "Will the grey come back? No." Answer the restock question directly.
  3. A time-boxed perk, e.g. 7pm, first 5 orders get free next-day. A perk, not a discount (Zororo's £10, 10-hat drop did 7.5x).
- **Biggest mistake:** fake scarcity. "Selling fast" with no number, or a "last chance" that comes back, kills trust for good. Only post true stock numbers, and only say "no restock" if you mean it.

**Rule across all three:** TOFU earns the follow, MOFU earns the trust, BOFU only works once the first two are done.

## 2. Pillars of a high-performing short-form video

Hook, retention, payoff and CTA, plus two the teardown showed matter: the send trigger and the loop.

### 1. Hook (0–2s)

- **What it is:** the first frame, line and on-screen text that make a scroller stop.
- **Why it works:** people decide in about a second whether something is "for me". The algorithm sees that as a low early swipe-away rate, and it's the gate to everything else.
- **Mistake:** a logo, a slow pan or "Hey guys" first. The interesting bit has to be in frame one.

### 2. Retention (2s to the payoff)

- **What it is:** a question left open, plus a change every 1–3 seconds, so leaving feels like missing something.
- **Why it works:** an unanswered question nags at people. Average watch time and completion are among the strongest ranking signals.
- **Mistake:** answering the hook too early. Once they know the ending, they're gone.

### 3. Payoff

- **What it is:** the moment that delivers what the hook promised: the punchline, the reveal or the score.
- **Why it works:** a satisfying ending makes the time feel well spent. It earns likes and follows, and tells the algorithm the video delivered.
- **Mistake:** hook-bait. Promising "you won't believe this" and delivering nothing trains people to scroll past you.

### 4. Send trigger (added)

- **What it is:** one moment built to make someone think "my mate does this".
- **Why it works:** sharing is how lads talk to each other. Shares push content to people who don't follow you, which is how a small account grows (Bulley's meme carousel got 17.7x its normal share rate).
- **Mistake:** content about the brand instead of about the viewer's life. Nobody sends an advert.

### 5. CTA

- **What it is:** one clear next action that matches the funnel stage.
- **Why it works:** people act when told exactly what to do. Comments, follows and profile visits are all signals the algorithm reads.
- **Mistake:** "like, follow, share, link in bio" all at once. One ask only; TOFU asks for a comment, BOFU for the link.

### 6. Loop (added)

- **What it is:** an ending that runs straight back into the start.
- **Why it works:** people rewatch without noticing. A rewatch counts as watch time above 100%.
- **Mistake:** a 3-second logo end card that kills the loop.

### Example script: "Rate the Fit, ep. 1" (about 35s)

| Time | Pillar | Shot / spoken | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | [HOOK] | Mic pushed at a lad on the high street, low angle. **You:** "Rate your fit out of ten, mate." | "rate the fit ep. 1" |
| 2–5s | [RETENTION] | **Him:** "Solid seven, that." His mate, off camera: "Six, max." | "him: 7 · his mate: 6 😭" |
| 5–14s | [RETENTION] | Quick cuts: he names each piece with a close-up of each. Jacket, trainers, then a faded old cap. **His mate:** "Mate, the cap's letting you down." | "the cap tho…" |
| 14–18s | [SEND TRIGGER] | **His mate:** "He's had that cap since Year 9." Both laugh. | "since year 9 💀" |
| 18–22s | [RETENTION] | **You:** "Swap it. Put this on." Hand over the grey Deira cap in slow-mo. | "one swap." |
| 22–28s | [PAYOFF] | He adjusts it and checks his reflection in a shop window. **You:** "Now what?" **Him:** "Nah… that's a nine." **His mate:** "Different gravy." | "7 → 9 🧢" |
| 28–32s | [CTA] | **You:** "Rate his fit in the comments. Ep. 2 Monday." | "rate it 👇 follow for ep. 2" |
| 32–35s | [LOOP] | **His mate:** "Now rate MY fit." Hard cut back to frame one. | none |

**Caption:** "one cap, two points 😭 rate him below 👇 #ratethefit #ukstreetwear"

The cap's price is never mentioned. This is TOFU; the link-in-bio push comes later in the funnel.

## 3. Hook types

| # | Hook type | Template | Why it stops the scroll | Example 1 | Example 2 | Stage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | POV | "POV: you [relatable situation] in [setting]" | The viewer puts themselves in the scene before they've decided to watch. | "POV: you turn up to five-a-side thinking you're different" | "POV: your mum says you're not wearing that cap to Nan's" | TOFU |
| 2 | Callout | "If you [habit], [stop / this one's for you]." | Hearing your own habit named feels personal, like it's aimed at you. | "If you bend your brim like a banana, stop." | "Lads who've had the same cap since Year 9 — this is for you." | TOFU |
| 3 | Question | "Would you [choice]?" / "[A] or [B]?" | The brain answers a question automatically, and the answer goes in the comments. | "Would you model for Deira?" | "Cap forwards or backwards, lad?" | TOFU |
| 4 | Curiosity gap | "Wait for [the moment]" / "Watch what happens when [X]." | An open loop people need closed. | "Wait for the ref." | "Watch what happens when I pull the stitching." | TOFU / MOFU |
| 5 | Hot take | "[Opinion half your audience disagrees with]." | People stay to argue, and arguing means comments. | "Backwards caps should be illegal." | "A £28 cap does more for your fit than £100 trainers." | TOFU |
| 6 | Scarcity | "We made [real number]. There's [real number] left." | A hard limit turns browsing into a decision. | "We made 28 grey caps. There's [X] left." | "7pm. Five slots. That's it." | BOFU |
| 7 | Versus | "[Cheap option] vs [ours] — spot the difference." | It's a game, and everyone wants to guess right. | "£8 cap vs £28 cap. Spot it." | "Grey or black — which is tidier?" | MOFU |
| 8 | Story opener | "[Unexpected thing] just happened." | People want to know how a story ends. | "Someone just bought a cap at 2am. Who are you?" | "A lad stopped me on the bus to ask about this cap." (only if it happened) | MOFU |
| 9 | Number | "[N] [mistakes / rules / fits] every lad [does]." | A set number promises a quick, complete payoff. | "Three cap mistakes every lad makes." | "One cap. Five fits. Go." | TOFU |
| 10 | Reply to a comment | "Replying to @[user]: '[their comment]'" | A real comment on screen is social proof, and it feels like an ongoing conversation. | "Replying: 'day 1 of asking for a free cap'" | "Replying: 'why's it £28 tho?'" | MOFU |

### The 3 to prioritise now

1. **POV.** It's the natural opener for skits, which were the strongest pattern in the teardown (8.8x). It lands on lads who've never heard of you, which is the job at your size.
2. **Question.** Participation hooks averaged 7.2x, and Bulley's question hook did 12x. It also fits the street interviews your TikTok already does well, and the comments it gets feed the algorithm.
3. **Scarcity.** It's the only one of the ten that directly sells the 28 greys, and Zororo got 7.5x with it on a cap of the same kind. It only works with real numbers; one fake "last chance" and it's dead.
