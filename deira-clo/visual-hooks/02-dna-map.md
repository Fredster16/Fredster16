## E. Overused mechanisms (flagged; lower priority for new ideas)

1. **Left-vs-right comparison (5/10).** It's the default layout of the set. New concepts should avoid it unless they have a specific reason.
2. **Meaning that depends on the text (7/10)**, mostly on plain white backgrounds (6/10). Seven of the ten jokes only land once the words are read, and only S02 works without them.
3. **Romance or dating as the set-up (5/10).** It's also where the two highest-risk seeds sit: S09 for audience age, and S04/S10 for saturation.
4. **AI black-silhouette illustration (4/10).** It's a style rather than a mechanism, but it's the most repeated surface in the set.
5. **Fill-in-the-blank chat (S04 + S10).** Only 2 seeds, but they're near-duplicates of each other, and the format is already used by other brands.

## F. Underexplored mechanisms (gaps to guide discovery)

| Gap | Why it matters for Deira (from your brief) |
|---|---|
| **Cap as the slide-1 subject** (0/10) | The cap is your only product and the logo is your "signature", yet none of the seed mechanisms make the product the thing that stops the scroll |
| **Physical, real-world set-ups**: props, stunts, locations (0/10). Every seed is a graphic, a screenshot or a found photo | Your brief lists your photography style as "studio and street", and none of the seeds use either |
| **Quality proof**: stitching, materials, construction (0/10) | Quality is your customers' #1 buying priority; you list cotton twill, stitched |
| **Price shown visually** (0/10). S01 mentions saving money but never shows a price | Price is your differentiator and your customers' #2 priority |
| **House music** (0/10) | Your long-term link and both owners' passion |
| **Football and British lad culture** (0/10). Seed settings are US hotels, US cars and Brazilian snacks | Listed customer interests |
| **Mechanisms that work with no text** (1/10, only S02) | The set is fragile: every hook fails if the label isn't read |
| **Optical or perspective tricks, scale, transformation, deconstruction, stress tests, platform mechanics (e.g. the swipe as camera movement)** (0/10) | Entire mechanism families missing |
| **Owners and community on camera** (0/10) | Your brief says "why us" is direct contact with the owners and a tight-knit community |

These gaps set the brief for Phase 3: physical, product-led, UK, mechanisms that work without words.
